Panel

Introducing Panel, the ultimate component designed to bring structure, style, and flexibility to your applications. With a dedicated header, body, and footer, it provides a seamless way to organize content while maintaining a sleek and modern aesthetic.

Key features:
  • ✅ Modular & Intuitive – Easily configure and customize each section to fit your design.
  • 🎨 3 + Tailwind Ready – A perfect blend of Design principles with Tailwind’s flexibility.
  • ⚡ Seamless Integration – Works effortlessly with components for a consistent UI experience.
  • 🛠 Highly Customizable – Add actions, icons, and interactive elements with ease.

Whether you're building dashboards, admin panels, or interactive UIs, Panel is your go-to solution for structured and elegant content presentation.
