Which plan should I choose?

Match the license to your delivery model

Choose Standard when

  • You are a solo developer or team working on one Angular product together.
  • You are building one Angular admin, dashboard, SaaS portal, or internal tool.
  • Your app ships on one production domain, including its subdomains.

Choose Professional when

  • Your team has multiple Angular developers or contractors.
  • You build more than one product, tenant app, agency client app, or domain.
  • You want priority support for production UI and template questions.
